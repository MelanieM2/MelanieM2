# About me
I'm a mathematician with a background in spectral geometry, geometric analysis on manifolds, and mathematical physics. I'm currently interested in exploring geometric aspects of deep learning while also developing my skills in applied mathematics, machine learning, data science, and AI.

A major part of this journey is strengthening my computer science and programming skills. This is why I'm here: to learn, experiment, often from scratch, and build along the way.

I'm also interested in science communication and AI-supported outreach in STEM, particularly in exploring how we can bring AI and Art together to make STEM fields more accessible and engaging while addressing important societal challenges.

**If you have questions, feedback, ideas, or would like to collaborate, feel free to get in touch! I'm always happy to connect, learn from others, and explore new ideas together over a ☕ or 🍵. 🙂**




# About this repository
This repository documents in form of small projects and experiments my learning journey, including, but not limited to, the following:
- Machine Learning (regression, classification, neural networks)
- Data analysis and prediction tasks
- Experimental deep learning (e.g. grokking in neural networks)
- AutoML and model evaluation workflows
- Simple Python projects involving neural networks, LLMs 
- Building more application-oriented AI prototypes
- AI-assisted programming
